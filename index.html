About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at KAIST. Previously, I was a Perception Algorithm Engineer on the Scene Team at WeRide, where I worked on traffic-light perception and operational design domain (ODD) classification for autonomous driving. I received my M.S. degree in Cyberspace Security from Beihang University (BUAA) in 2024 and my B.S. degree from the same university in 2021.

My research focuses on world models, autonomous driving, embodied AI, and trustworthy machine learning. I am particularly interested in how learning systems can represent uncertainty, anticipate decision-relevant consequences, and make recoverable decisions in complex dynamic environments.
