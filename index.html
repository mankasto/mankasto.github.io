About Me

I am an incoming Ph.D. student at KAIST. I currently work as a Perception Algorithm Engineer at WeRide, where I develop traffic-light detection and state-estimation systems for autonomous driving. I received my M.S. degree in Cyberspace Security from Beihang University (BUAA) in 2024 and my B.S. degree from the same university in 2021.

My research focuses on world models, autonomous driving, embodied AI, and trustworthy machine learning. I am particularly interested in how learning systems can represent uncertainty, anticipate decision-relevant consequences, and make recoverable decisions in complex dynamic environments.
