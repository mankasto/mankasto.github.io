# High-resolution raster framework prompt

## Scientific message

The figure communicates six progressively stronger capabilities for safety-critical embodied world models:

**Generation → Modeling → Understanding → Reasoning → Decision → Verification**

The capability order, labels, and actor ownership are fixed. Candidate actions belong to the embodied agent; the displayed futures are their consequences. Verification independently checks evidence and constraints before execution and does not imply a formal safety guarantee.

## Reference brief

The visual design was calibrated against overview figures from recent world-model surveys:

- *A Comprehensive Survey on World Models for Embodied AI* — borrow the integration of compact technical evidence with a unified framework; reject its manuscript-outline content.
- *A Survey of World Models for Autonomous Driving* — borrow the progression from multimodal scene modeling toward planning; reject its paper-specific taxonomy.
- *The Role of World Models in Shaping Autonomous Driving* — borrow the observed-scene / controllable-future visual grammar; reject modality and ecosystem inventories.
- *World Models for Autonomous Driving: An Initial Survey* — borrow the environment–prediction–action relationship; reject implementation-specific module names.

No scientific modules, labels, or claims were imported from these references.

## Generation prompt

Use case: scientific-educational.

Asset type: high-resolution landscape publication thumbnail / graphical abstract for an academic personal website.

Create a polished raster scientific framework figure for “World Models for Safety-Critical Embodied Systems.” Use one continuous left-to-right storyline with the exact headings “Generation,” “Modeling,” “Understanding,” “Reasoning,” “Decision,” and “Verification,” rendered once each in that order.

- Generation: several plausible future frames from the same observed scene.
- Modeling: structured spatiotemporal state using bird’s-eye view, occupancy, and trajectories.
- Understanding: objects, agents, relations, physical constraints, traffic rules, and affordance cues.
- Reasoning: action-conditioned counterfactual branches with visibly different risk consequences.
- Decision: safe selection, uncertainty-aware deferral, and unsafe-action rejection.
- Verification: independent evidence, rule, constraint, and uncertainty checks before execution.

Ground the framework in two coherent embodied contexts: autonomous driving at an urban intersection and robotic manipulation near a human. Use one consistent refined semi-realistic 2.5D technical-illustration family. Favor a compact 16:9 composition recognizable as a 190 × 120 thumbnail. Use meaningful technical evidence rather than generic icons.

Visual system: clean near-white background; dark navy structure; cyan-blue for perception/modeling; amber only for uncertainty and risk comparison; teal for verified safety; tiny red only for unsafe futures. Use crisp bold sans-serif headings, clean short arrows, controlled depth and ambient occlusion, and strong but restrained academic hierarchy.

Avoid manuscript outlines, section numbers, paper titles inside the image, benchmarks, method names, citations, equations, author names, metrics, decorative neural networks, generic brain icons, logos, watermarks, dashboard chrome, excessive gradients, neon, clutter, tiny prose, duplicated headings, or a disconnected card wall.
